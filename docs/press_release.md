# RoomLight  
## Design the Lighting Once. Sync It to Every Room.

RoomLight is built for hotels that want to improve guest satisfaction while reducing technical complexity by turning room lighting into a standardized, intuitive system across the entire property.

Lighting is one of the first things a guest interacts with, yet it often becomes a source of confusion. Too many switches. Unclear labels. Bedside buttons controlling distant lamps. What should feel effortless instead creates friction.

Behind the scenes, technical teams manage years of renovations and inconsistent wiring layouts. Each room type develops its own logic. Maintenance becomes reactive and scaling changes across floors is slow and costly.

RoomLight simplifies both experiences.

> “Lighting should support comfort, not distract from it. We built RoomLight to improve the guest experience while giving technical managers a system that is predictable, scalable, and easy to maintain.”  
> — RoomLight Product Team

A small number of clearly labeled smart panels replace traditional switch clusters. Controls use plain language such as *Entry*, *Toilet*, or *Reading Light*. Every light can be controlled from any panel, and the logic remains consistent across room types, so guests immediately understand how the room works.

---

Hotels define the lighting structure once per room type and replicate it everywhere. Standardized wireless light modules reduce installation variability and make pairing straightforward.

RoomLight also enables centralized control of every connected room. Hotels can automatically turn off all lights after checkout, prepare lighting scenes before guest arrival, and monitor energy usage from a single interface.

Getting started does not require a full renovation. Hotels can begin with a pilot floor, define their preferred lighting structure, and expand gradually across the property.

> “Before RoomLight, every renovation added more switches and more confusion. Now our rooms feel consistent, our guests rarely ask about lighting, and our maintenance team works with one clear system.”  
> — Technical Manager, Nordic City Hotel

With RoomLight, lighting becomes an asset rather than an afterthought.  
A better experience for guests. A simpler system for operations.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How is RoomLight different from traditional smart lighting systems?
RoomLight is designed specifically for hotel standardization, focusing on consistent control logic and scalable deployment across many rooms and properties.

### Does installation require major rewiring?
No. The system uses standardized wireless light modules that can be powered with already existing wiring, and smart panels that minimize structural changes.

### Can it scale across multiple properties?
Yes. Lighting templates can be replicated across floors, buildings, and entire hotel chains.

### What operational benefits can hotels expect?
Fewer guest support calls, reduced maintenance troubleshooting time, and improved energy management through centralized automation.
